# Writing style

Plain, specific and short. Before you send, ask what makes the text read as machine-written, and fix that.

- State facts and numbers. Cut a sentence that could appear unchanged in another project's notes.
- Plain words: "is" and "has", not "serves as" or "boasts"; "use", not "leverage" or "utilize". Never: additionally,
  crucial, delve, enhance, foster, garner, interplay, intricate, pivotal, showcase, testament, underscore, vibrant.
- No em dashes or en dashes. End the sentence or use a comma. Colons only before a list or an example.
- No filler ("in order to", "it is important to note"), no hedging stacks, no chatbot phrases, no closing summary
  that says nothing.
- No "not just X, but Y", no forced groups of three, no -ing tails ("ensuring...", "highlighting...").
- Active voice that names the actor. One idea per sentence; split one the reader has to parse twice.
- Whole sentences with articles and verbs. No arrows or symbol-speak in prose.
- Sentence-case headings, straight quotes, no emojis, bold only where it helps the reader find something.
